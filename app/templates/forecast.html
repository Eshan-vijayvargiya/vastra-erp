How the forecast works

1 · Daily sales per category

Every order line is added up by day and category: two years, about 740 days each.

2 · Calendar features

Month of year, Sunday, and festival windows: days before Diwali (sarees, dhoti & safi), before 26 Jan and 15 Aug (flags), and the wedding season.

3 · Ridge regression

Least squares with a small penalty so a dozen-plus weights can be learned safely. Older days are scaled up by year-on-year growth so the forecast is at today's level.

4 · What it learned

The last week before Diwali adds about {{ drivers.diwali | rupees }} a day of saree orders; the week before 26 Jan or 15 Aug adds {{ drivers.natday | rupees }} a day of flags; and a Sunday cuts fabric orders by about {{ (-drivers.sunday) | rupees }} a day.
